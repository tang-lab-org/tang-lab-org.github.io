Our lab has been awarded a DISC Graduate Student Award from the Data Institute for Societal Challenges (DISC) at the University of Oklahoma for the project titled "Deep-learning-Assisted Photoacoustic and Ultrasound Evaluation for Pre-transplant Human Liver Viability."

Congratulations to the team on this achievement! We look forward to advancing this research and exploring its potential for future translational and clinical applications.
