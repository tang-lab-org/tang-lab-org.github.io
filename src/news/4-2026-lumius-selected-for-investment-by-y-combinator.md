<h3>We are excited to share that Dr. Tri Vu, a member of our lab, together with Luca Menozzi and Dr. Chenhang Li, has been selected for investment by Y Combinator, one of the most prestigious startup incubators in the world.</h3>

<h3>Their startup, Lumius (YC X26), focuses on translating ultrasound innovations into impactful clinical applications, joining a distinguished group of companies such as OpenAI and Airbnb.</h3>

<h3>Congratulations on this outstanding achievement. We are proud of Dr. Vu and look forward to Lumius's continued success.</h3>
