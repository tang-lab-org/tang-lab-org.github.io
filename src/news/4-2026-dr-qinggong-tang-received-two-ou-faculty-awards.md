Dr. Qinggong Tang, Stephenson School of Biomedical Engineering, Gallogly College of Engineering, has been recognized with two University of Oklahoma faculty honors in 2026: the Nancy L. Mergler Faculty Mentor Award for Undergraduate Research and the Award for Excellence in Research in Engineering and Applied Science.

These recognitions highlight Dr. Tang's contributions to undergraduate mentorship and research in biomedical engineering.
